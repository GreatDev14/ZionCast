Zero Headache Setup

Ready in Under 60 Seconds

Follow these 3 straightforward steps to configure your sanctuary projector or TV display with ZionCast.

1

Connect TV via HDMI

Plug your HDMI or DisplayPort cable into your church TV or projector. Press Win + P on your keyboard and choose "Extend" so the TV acts as a second monitor.

💡 Pro Tip: Never duplicate screens. Extending allows your operator to work unseen.
2

Select Video Input

Launch ZionCast and navigate to the Live Video tab. Select your USB webcam, DroidCam wireless mobile camera, or HDMI capture card directly from the dropdown.

📱 DroidCam connects over Wi-Fi or USB tethering for high-definition mobile streaming.
3

Launch TV Output

Click "Launch TV Output" on the top toolbar. ZionCast automatically opens the live display window on Display 2 full-screen. Select any song or scripture and tap ▶ GO LIVE.

✨ Real-time font slider lets you adjust lyrics dynamically on the live TV.
